Prayer for Growth & Character

Lord, as I grow older, I pray that I may be known as:

⏳ Available ▼
rather than overly busy or self-focused.
❤️ Compassionate ▼
more than confident.
⚓ Content ▼
not driven by ambition or comparison.
🗣️ Encouraging ▼
more than critical.
🤝 Empathetic ▼
striving to deeply understand and share in others' pain and joy.
🛡️ Faithful ▼
in all that I say and do.
🕊️ Forgiving ▼
rather than holding grudges.
🤲 Generous ▼
giving my time and resources rather than merely accumulating wealth.
🍃 Gentle ▼
choosing kindness and strength over dominance or intimidation.
👂 A listener ▼
valuing understanding over the need to be heard.
💝 Loving ▼
prioritizing grace over the need to be right.
🌾 Meek ▼
showing strength through humility.
🧱 Reliable ▼
seeking to be trustworthy rather than chasing fame or recognition.
🕯️ Sacrificial ▼
valuing service over mere achievement.
🛑 Self-controlled ▼
prioritizing wisdom over impulsive desires.
💡 Thoughtful ▼
using wisdom and care in all decisions.
☤ Stewardship & Servant Leadership ▼
Taking care of what we have been given, using our talents to serve others, and actively working to leave things better for future generations.
💎 Integrity (Honesty & Humility) ▼
Walking truthfully according to biblical principles, doing the right thing when no one is looking, and maintaining a humble heart that puts others first.
☀️ Joyful Resilience & Positivity ▼
Meeting life's challenges with a positive attitude, choosing joy, and leaning on the strength to bounce back when things get chaotic or tough.
🌊 Peace & Patience ▼
Cultivating a calm, reassuring atmosphere in the home where everyone is quick to listen, slow to speak, and committed to creating a safe haven for one another.
⚖️ Self-Control ▼
Practicing the daily discipline to govern our own actions, emotions, and responses, keeping our boundaries strong and our faith active.
